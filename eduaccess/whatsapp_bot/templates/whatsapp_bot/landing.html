What is EduAccess?

EduAccess is an AI-powered study assistant built specifically for secondary school students. It covers the two subjects that matter most in exams — Maths and English — and acts like a patient, always-available tutor.

You can type a question in plain language — "explain passive voice", "solve 3x + 6 = 18", "what is a fraction?" — and get a clear, step-by-step answer instantly. No waiting, no judgement, no cost.

EduAccess also works on WhatsApp, so students who do not have a smartphone app can still get the same tutor experience through a platform they already use every day.

Students without a private tutor

Students with limited internet

Download study packs and audio lessons to your phone once, then study them anywhere — even with no data or Wi-Fi connection.

Students preparing for exams

Practise topic-by-topic, get instant feedback on your answers, and track your score so you can see which areas need more work.

Students who learn by listening

Every study pack includes a spoken audio lesson you can listen to on the way to school or at any time that suits you.

What you can do

Ask the tutor any question

Type in plain language. "Explain algebra", "What is passive voice?", "How do I find a percentage?" — get a clear answer every time.

Practise with real questions

Get Maths and English practice questions, submit your answer, and receive instant feedback on whether you were right and why.

Download packs for offline study

Save complete topic guides — notes, examples, and practice questions — to your phone so you can study without any internet.

Listen to audio lessons

Every topic pack comes with a full spoken lesson. Play it on the bus, at home, or anywhere — no reading required.

Track your progress

Your scores are saved to your account after every practice session so you can see what you have improved and what to focus on next.
